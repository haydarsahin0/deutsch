# Viral-Short #1 – „Deutsch wörtlich genommen“ (original Short, not cut from an episode)

## Idea
Melo draws German compound words LITERALLY. Every beat is about 3.5 s: the big word on top with a level badge, then the absurd literal ink illustration, then a punchline. Levels climb A1 → C1, so viewers stay to see the C1 word. It ends with a comment question and loops back to frame 1.

## Why it can go viral
1. The first frame is an absurd image (Melo with shoes on his hands), which stops the scroll with no intro.
2. It works muted and for every level, Germans included, so the audience is about 10× bigger than a B1–C1 grammar Short.
3. The A1→C1 escalation is a retention device; 7 beats in about 30 s give a high completion and rewatch rate.
4. Comment bait everyone can answer („Welches Wort fehlt?“ → Eichhörnchen, Schmetterling, Fremdschämen …).
5. It is shareable („schick das jemandem, der Deutsch lernt“) and can become a series (Teil 2/3).

## Production
- Owner records ONE ElevenLabs part (about 30 s).
- New: 7 literal ink illustrations (one Opus agent), a mini script in cue format, render, then `make-short.py` with the big word on top.
- Hook rule: frame 1 = Handschuh picture + word, spoken in the first 0.3 s.
- Hand-made cover: Melo with shoes on his hands + glowing pear, „DEUTSCH WÖRTLICH 😂“.
